# Page templates

Every template starts with SiteHeader and ends with SiteFooter. No two sections on a page share a layout family.

## Home (built)

1. Hero: asymmetric split, arched photo.
2. BookingWidget bar, just under the hero.
3. Welcome: hosts' copy beside an offset two-photo collage.
4. Rooms: RoomCard featured plus compact stack.
5. AmenityBento.
6. PhotoBand: breakfast.
7. Reviews: score, featured quote, scroll-snap row.
8. LocationBlock: map slot and travel-time tiles.
9. ClosingCTA.

## Room detail

1. Gallery.
2. Two columns from `bp-md`: name, AvailabilityPill, facts, description, grouped in-room amenities, house rules on the left; the sticky BookingWidget panel on the right. On phones the panel becomes a bottom bar with the price and "Check availability".
3. Reviews that mention this room.
4. Other rooms as compact RoomCards.

## Rooms

1. Page title (`display-l`) and one intro line.
2. Optional filters: dates and guests.
3. RoomCards: featured plus compact, then a comparison of sleeps, bed, view and rate.

## Booking

1. Your stay summary with every line item and the cancellation policy.
2. Guest details: name, email, phone, arrival time, requests. Labels above, errors below.
3. Confirmation: reference, dates, what happens next, how to reach the hosts.

## Around us

1. Title and intro.
2. LocationBlock with the full list.
3. Things to do: a bento of photo cells with distance and a host tip.
4. Getting here by air, train and road.

## Contact and FAQ

1. Copyable contact details, WhatsApp and a short form.
2. FAQ accordion: check-in, breakfast, children, pets, parking, payment, cancellation.
