# Gallery

The five-photo mosaic at the top of each room page, opening a full-screen lightbox.

- **Provide:** at least five photos with real alt text, strongest first.
- The lead photo takes the left half with `radius-arch` on its top corners; the rest are `radius-lg`.
- On phones it shows three photos and the "All photos" button.
- The lightbox shows "3 of 24", supports arrow keys and swipe, and closes on Escape.
