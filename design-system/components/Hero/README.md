# Hero

The home page opening: an asymmetric split with the copy on the left and one arched photo on the right.

- **Provide:** one landscape photo of the villa (at least 1600 x 1200, warm daylight), a headline of 8 words or fewer, subtext of 20 words or fewer, and the two CTAs.
- At most four text elements: one eyebrow, the headline (`display-xl`, max 2 lines), the subtext (`body-l`), the CTA row. No badges, trust strips or taglines inside the hero.
- The photo uses `radius-arch` on its top corners. On phones the photo stacks above the copy.
- Top padding never exceeds `space-24`. Size to content, never `100vh`.
- The copy enters once on load with a short stagger; it is static under reduced motion.
