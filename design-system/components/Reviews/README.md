# Reviews

The overall score, one featured quote and a scroll-snap row of short reviews.

- **Provide:** the real average score and review count with their source, one standout quote, and three to six short reviews with the guest's first name, home city and month of stay.
- Quotes are 3 lines at most and use typographic quote marks.
- Stars are `coral` and the score is always written as text.
- Every quote, score and name in the previews is a sample. Use only real reviews, with permission or from the booking site they came from.
